**Investigation ideas**
- app_logs: (1) error rate by service and 5-minute bucket, (2) p95 latency_ms by service and status_code, (3) services whose errors spike while latency stays flat.
- e2e_automate: (1) `k8s_container_restart_count` and crash loops by pod, (2) error-level logs by namespace and app label, (3) slowest `method`/`took` pairs per deploy revision.
- default (traces): (1) top operations by p95 duration, (2) spans with span_status ERROR grouped by service and peer_service, (3) trace_id fan-out to find services inflating the critical path.
- e2e_secret_row: (1) confirm no live credentials in `token` outside test rows, (2) retention and access review for the stream, (3) flag the field for redaction at ingest.
- e2e_jk_logs_*: (1) drop or retire duplicates to cut clutter, (2) one representative stream kept for E2E smoke tests, (3) level distribution check to confirm fixtures behave.
- triggers: (1) delivery_attempted=false or retries>0 grouped by module, (2) actual_value vs threshold_value drift per alert, (3) evaluation_took_in_secs outliers.
- _o2_alert_calibration_digests: (1) skip_count trends per rule_id, (2) coverage_gap=true frequency, (3) alerts_in_scope vs finding_count ratio.
- _o2_service_verdicts: (1) regressed verdicts by service and to_version, (2) consecutive_regressed >= 2 as a deploy-regression signal, (3) traces vs RUM vs ns_consecutive_drop correlation per deploy.
- _rumdata: (1) top error_message by view_url, (2) crash rate per browser and version, (3) sessions with errors vs total sessions per day.
- traces_service_graph_request_total / _failed_total: (1) failure rate by client→server edge, (2) edges with traffic but no server span (unresolved), (3) slowdown on one edge after a version change.
- _o2_service_requests_total: (1) 5xx share by service and version, (2) traffic shift after deploy using where_key, (3) request drop per env.
- _o2_service_endpoint_duration_seconds_bucket: (1) p99 by operation, (2) endpoints degrading across versions, (3) tail-latency outliers with low traffic.
