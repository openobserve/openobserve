How uptime is measured

  • A day only loses uptime while a confirmed incident was open for that component — brief blips that never became an incident do not count against it.
  • Degraded performance is shown on the timeline but does not reduce the uptime percentage.
  • Scheduled maintenance appears in blue and is excluded from uptime.
  • Days filled in from historical records (backfilled) are estimates and are marked as such.
